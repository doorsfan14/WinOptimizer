# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

WinOptimizer contributors are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free project space for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or viewpoint.

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include clear and considerate communication, constructive feedback, accepting corrections, and focusing on what is best for users. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, intimidation, discriminatory language, personal attacks, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's private information without permission.

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate them. Maintainers will communicate the reason for moderation when practical.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Do not post private or identifying information in a public issue. Maintainers will review reports promptly and handle them with discretion.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is officially representing the project. It is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, available at <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html>.
